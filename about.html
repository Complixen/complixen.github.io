Our Story

The industry forgot about startups. So we built a new model.

If you are a growing startup, the traditional cybersecurity industry gives you two terrible options:

Option One

The bloated platform

Buy a massive, expensive all in one platform filled with features you aren't ready for, which takes months to deploy and slows down your engineers.

Option Two

The legacy consultants

Hire legacy consultants who drop long PDF reports on your desk, offer zero technical help, and charge massive retainer fees.

The market didn't need another monolithic tool. It needed surgical, specialized solutions. That is why we built our company on the principle of modularity.

Our Thesis

Modularity over bloat

We don't believe in forcing you into a single pane of glass that does everything poorly. Instead, we built dedicated, purpose built platforms and services for the exact bottlenecks startups face.
