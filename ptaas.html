The Problem

Automated scanners aren't enough. Legacy pentesting is a black box.

If you are scaling a startup, you need to prove your security posture to enterprise buyers. But current solutions leave you stuck between flawed options:

Flawed option 01

The Scanner Gap

Many firms sell pentests that are strictly automated vulnerability scans. They completely miss business logic flaws, authorization bypasses, and complex attack chains.

Flawed option 02

The Black Box Bottleneck

Traditional manual pentesting firms disappear for weeks, giving you zero visibility into their testing progress or findings until the very end.

Flawed option 03

The Static PDF Problem

When the manual test is finally done, you receive a static report that your developers hate, which becomes outdated the moment you push a new code update.

You need the depth of an expert engineer, delivered through a transparent, modern platform.

The Solution

Real world security expertise, managed in one centralized tool

Human led testing with the visibility and workflow of a modern platform.

What we call it

Beyond the Automated Scan

While scanners find simple bugs, our experienced security engineers manually simulate advanced attacks. We find the complex business logic flaws and chained vulnerabilities that could actually compromise your platform.

What we call it

The PTaaS Dashboard

Say goodbye to waiting weeks for a PDF. Our dedicated PTaaS platform gives you live visibility into the pentest as it happens. Watch verified findings roll in, track remediation status, and sync everything directly to Jira.

What we call it

One Click Fix Validation

When your team pushes a patch, you shouldn't have to pay for another full pentest. Simply request a retest directly through the platform, and our engineers will manually validate your remediations so you can close the loop instantly.
