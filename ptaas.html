Automated scanners aren't enough. Legacy pentesting is a black box.

If you are scaling a startup, you need to prove your security posture to enterprise buyers. But current solutions leave you stuck between two flawed options:

/ 01

The Scanner Gap

Many firms sell pentests that are strictly automated vulnerability scans. They completely miss business logic flaws, authorization bypasses, and complex attack chains.

/ 02

The Black-Box Bottleneck

Traditional manual pentesting firms disappear for weeks, giving you zero visibility into their testing progress or findings until the very end.

/ 03

The Static PDF Problem

When the manual test is finally done, you receive a 100-page static report that your developers hate, which becomes outdated the moment you push a new code update.

You need the depth of an expert engineer, delivered through a transparent, modern platform.
